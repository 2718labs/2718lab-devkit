# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

2718lab is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming
community. Participation must remain safe regardless of experience, identity,
background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect project scope, contributor time, privacy, and security boundaries.
- Keep examples and logs redacted to the minimum needed for collaboration.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, contributors, or the
  integrity of the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, credential exposure, and
publication of private communications are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a `2718labs` organization owner. Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate
action to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical. Retaliation
against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation of this code.
