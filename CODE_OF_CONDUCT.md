# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and
safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and respect.
- Keep reviews focused on code, contracts, and outcomes rather than people.
- Protect private data, credentials, and the safety boundaries of host systems.
- Accept good-faith feedback and correct mistakes promptly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
exposure of private information, and attempts to bypass a safety boundary are
not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report an issue privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting channel when appropriate, or contact the
maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are handled
confidentially and may result in a warning, removal of content, temporary
restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
